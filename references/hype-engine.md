# Hype Engine: Attention Recovery Loop

Use this file when the user asks how to make an event spread, how Sun-style
promotion works, or how to turn attention into a recoverable asset. The target
must be the user's own project, product, content, or a lawful public-interest
analysis. Do not use it to harass, fabricate, or attack an identifiable person.

## Seven-Stage Loop

1. **目标**: name the specific recoverable result, not "make it viral".
2. **受众**: who already cares, who is adjacent, and what they already believe.
3. **钩子**: one claim, contrast, or question that creates a reason to share.
4. **情绪负载**: which feeling carries the message, and which feeling would
   later turn against the promoter.
5. **分发向量**: where the first 1,000 viewers, first 100 shares, and first
   media pickup come from.
6. **转化点**: what action turns attention into cash, signup, distribution, or
   reusable content.
7. **退出与冷却**: what happens when the spike ends, and who still holds a
   usable asset.

## Stage Questions

| Stage | Sharp question |
|---|---|
| 目标 | What asset exists after the attention dies? |
| 受众 | Which group pays attention at the lowest acquisition cost? |
| 钩子 | Is the hook provable, or only deniable? |
| 情绪 | Does the emotion create followers or enemies? |
| 分发 | Which platform gives the best first move? |
| 转化 | What is the actual call to action? |
| 退出 | Is this a one-cycle spike or a reusable channel? |

## Attention Accounting

Split every hype outcome into three buckets:

- **潜在回收**: reach, options, narrative inventory not yet converted.
- **已实现回收**: verified cash, signup, contract, or distribution.
- **不可回收**: spent credibility, lost trust, moderation, or legal exposure.

Attention without a conversion point is inventory with no buyer. When traffic
rises while trust and exit balances fall, the promoter is liquidating
reputation, not building an asset.

## Red Lines

- Do not fabricate proof, screenshots, chats, transfers, or endorsements.
- Do not impersonate a real person or brand.
- Do not use private, sexual, medical, family, or non-consensual material as
  the payload.
- Do not promise guaranteed returns, token price movement, or automatic
  "win-back" outcomes.
- Label paid promotion, conflicts, and staged content where required.

The sustainable version of "吃干抹净" is to capture full legitimate value and
leave a reason to transact again. A hype loop that only harvests once is a
burn event, not a strategy.
