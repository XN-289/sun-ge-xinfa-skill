placed on the evidence ladder before use.

## Evidence Rules

- The source repository's existence proves distribution, not the truth of its private-life claims.
- The essay's fiction label is part of the text, not a shield that converts readers' factual claims back into fiction.
- Do not copy the full TeX, PDF, screenshots, or private passages into this repository.
- Do not treat community adaptations as first-party sources.
- The source repository has no license; quote only the minimum necessary for commentary and link to the original.
- Keep the analysis anchored to public mechanisms: attention, optionality, asymmetry, deniability, and reputation cost.
