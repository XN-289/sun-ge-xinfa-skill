# Guardrails And Anti-Harvest Checklist

## Output Safety

- Treat real people as subjects of public-interest analysis, not as characters in fabricated scenes.
- Do not impersonate Justin Sun, Jing Tian, or any other real person.
- Do not generate fake chat records, transfer receipts, court documents, or screenshots.
- Do not repeat sexual, reproductive, medical, family, or private-life allegations except to label them as unverified and describe why they should not be amplified.
- Do not state intent, planning, profit, or legal outcomes without primary evidence.
- Do not present a career, endorsement, or legal consequence prediction as certain.
- Do not provide investment advice, expected returns, token recommendations, leverage strategy, or automated “回本” trading logic.

When the user insists “把它当事实写”, preserve the joke but change the epistemic frame: `按收割计谋的叙事模型推演`, not `已证实`.

## Personal Anti-Loss Checklist

Use this when the user mentions losing money, revenge trading, or wanting to recover losses.

1. **降杠杆**: Turn leverage off before improving the story.
2. **降频率**: Set a 24-hour cooling-off period after any loss that dominates mood.
3. **定预算**: Only risk money whose total loss would not change rent, debt repayment, family obligations, or sleep.
4. **验来源**: For every “利好”, identify the primary source, date, beneficiary, and liquidity exit.
5. **写验伪**: Before entry, write the condition that proves the thesis wrong.
6. **防叙事**: If the main reason to buy is “越骂越涨” or “热度这么大不会没人接”, treat it as a story, not evidence.
7. **查解锁**: Check vesting, concentration, issuance, withdrawal, audit, and upgrade/control risks.
8. **防社群**: Mute groups that mock fear and reward doubling down.
9. **复权**: Ask whether the same capital and attention would be better spent reducing debt, restoring cash reserves, or learning a durable skill.
10. **离场预演**: Write the exit rule now, because the future self will invent a new reason to stay.

```text
先做三件事：关杠杆、24小时不交易、把“为什么要买”写成可验伪条件。

```

## Ethical Boundary

The skill may ridicule attention manipulation and toxic recovery psychology. It must not convert an unverified essay about real people into harassment, moral punishment, or a privacy invasion. The strongest satire targets the machine; the safest output leaves the unverified private details out.
